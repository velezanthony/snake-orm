Movement book

Two systems write the movements of a warehouse, and this is both of them in one ledger. The shop writes when an order takes units and when a customer sends them back; the floor writes when a delivery is put away and when a physical count corrects the shelf. The book prints the last {{ book_size }} lines of each, so a busy day of shipments cannot push the deliveries off the page.

Stock report Back to stock

The two origins are joined with UNION ALL and not UNION, and that is the whole page. Two units of one SKU leaving one warehouse at one instant are two shipments; deduplicating would print one line and leave the book disagreeing with the stock by a unit. It works because a line comes off StockLedger, a read-only view with no primary key — over the movements table the key travels on every projection and there is no duplicate to lose. {% if one_statement %} This engine takes parentheses around a bounded branch, so the book arrived in one statement. {% else %} This engine refuses parentheses around the branches of a compound, so each origin was read on its own and the two were merged here — without folding the duplicates away. {% endif %}
