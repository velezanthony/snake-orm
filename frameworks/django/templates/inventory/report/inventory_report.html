Where each row ranks inside its own warehouse

rank() over (partition by warehouse order by on_hand desc) — a WINDOW function. The position is a fact about the row's NEIGHBOURS, computed without collapsing them, which is what no filter and no GROUP BY could put in this column. Ties share a position.

Total so far, and total lately

sum(delta).over(order_by=...) is the RUNNING total — the default frame reaches from the start of the series to this row, so it only ever grows.

frame=snake_rows(snake_preceding(2), SNAKE_CURRENT_ROW) is the MOVING one: a window three movements wide that travels with the row. The two agree until the fourth movement of a pair and part company after it, which is why neither is worth showing on its own. Both come from the SAME statement.
