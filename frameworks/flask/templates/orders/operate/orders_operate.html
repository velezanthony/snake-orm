Settle

Issues the invoice, takes the money and ships the held units. The invoice is issued outside the savepoint and stands whatever follows; the payment, the shipment and the final state are inside it, so a declined card rewinds them and puts the units back on hold.

Cancel

A state and not a deletion: the order stays as history. From reserved it also gives the units back, which is why cancelling is not a boolean — from a draft there is nothing to give back, and inventing units the warehouse never had is the expensive kind of wrong.
